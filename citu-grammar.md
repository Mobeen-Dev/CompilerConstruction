Uniqueness in the GrammarThe grammar is unique because it uses Romanized Hindi/Urdu vocabulary as pseudocode keywords in place of standard English programming syntax. It replaces traditional control flow and operators with conversational phrases: jabtak: Acts as a while loop (literally translates to "as long as" or "until"). shro: Acts as a block opener like { or begin, derived from "shuru" (literally "start"). agar: Acts as an if condition (literally "if"). magar act as else and it must be inside agar khatam which it belongs
nikal: Acts as a break or exit() statement (literally "get out" or "leave"). khatam: Acts as a block closer like } or end (literally "finished" or "end"). medalo: Acts as an assignment operator =, used here as i medalo (i + 1) to mean i = i + 1 (literally "put in" or "insert").
